1. QUESTIONS & UNIT

Dual Decision Horizons

Pre-Departure (T-2h): Will the flight depart ≥15 min late?
Post-Departure: Will the flight arrive ≥15 min late?

One Row: One scheduled commercial flight leg operating between top hubs, joined with origin METAR weather & incoming aircraft turnaround history.
2. DATA & LEAKAGE

952k Cleaned Records

BTS TranStats + NOAA ASOS/METAR stations across 10 hubs (ATL, ORD, DFW, DEN, etc.) from Jan 2023 to Jul 2024.

Leakage Guard: Strict chronological split (Train: 2023–Apr 2024; Val: May 2024; Held-Out Test: Jun–Jul 2024). Weather merged strictly as-of T − 2 hours.
3. BENCHMARKS & METRIC

Calibrated GBDT

Evaluated against Majority-Class & Domain Rule baselines, Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and HistGradientBoosting.

Primary Metric: PR-AUC (Precision-Recall Area Under Curve), the gold standard for imbalanced aviation delays.
4. RESULTS & ANALYSIS

Statistically Significant

Arrival PR-AUC: 0.918 (F1: 0.836)
Departure PR-AUC: 0.566 (F1: 0.563)

Bootstrap test: Both models beat domain baselines with 95% CI strictly above zero (p < 0.001).
